TITLE

Leo and the Clever Bunny


STORY

Once upon a time, in a bright green forest, lived Leo the Lion. Leo was very big. Leo was very loud. And most of all, Leo had a very rumbly tummy. "Rumble, bumble, roar!" went Leo's tummy. "I am so hungry!" Leo groaned. "I want a big, tasty lunch!" Just then, a tiny rabbit named Barnaby hopped by. Barnaby was small, fluffy, and very clever. He loved to munch on sweet orange carrots. "Aha!" roared Leo, his eyes wide. "A fluffy little snack!" Leo sprang out of the bushes. "Stop right there, little bunny! You are my lunch!" Barnaby did not shake with fear. He stood tall on his tiptoes and smiled. "Oh, mighty Leo," Barnaby said in a sweet voice. "I would make a very small lunch. But did you know there is a much bigger lion nearby?" Leo stopped. He scratched his big mane. "A bigger lion? In my forest? No way!" "Yes, way!" squeaked Barnaby. "He is huge. He is mean. And he lives inside that deep, stone well over there." Leo's tummy rumbled loudly. "I will eat him instead! Show me this lion!" Barnaby led Leo to the old stone well. Leo marched proudly, stomping his big paws. "Stomp, stomp, stomp!" "Look down there," Barnaby whispered, pointing into the dark well. Leo leaned over the edge. He peered into the cool water. What did he see? He saw a big, furry face with sharp teeth looking right back at him! It was his own reflection, but Leo did not know that. "Hey! Get out of my well!" Leo roared. The lion in the well opened his mouth and seemed to roar back. "How dare you copy me!" Leo yelled, shaking his fist. The lion in the well shook his fist too. Leo was very angry. "I am the only king here!" he boomed. Without thinking, Leo took a giant leap. SPLASH! Leo jumped right into the well. Cold water splashed everywhere. Leo looked around, but there was no other lion. There was only wet, soggy water. "Oh dear," Leo splashed. "Where did he go?" Up above, Barnaby looked down and giggled. "You fought your own shadow, Leo! Next time, use your brain before you jump!" The well was not too deep, so Leo easily climbed out. He was wet, he was cold, and he looked like a soggy poodle. Barnaby waved his little paw. "Goodbye, Leo! Have a nice, wet day!" Barnaby hopped away happily to eat his carrots. Leo sighed, shook the water off his fur, and decided that maybe grass was a safer lunch today. From that day on, Leo always paused to think before he roared.


MORAL

Being smart and thinking before you act is much better than just being big and strong.
