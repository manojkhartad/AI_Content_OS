TITLE

Leo the Bossy and the Wiggling Wit


STORY

The Storyteller beamed, holding his glowing leather-bound book. "Welcome, little ones!" he cheered with a rhythmic skip. "And then, the most wonderful thing happened!"

Deep in the jungle lived Leo. Leo was a plump lion with a fluffy orange-brown mane and a royal purple velvet cape. He wore a small golden crown that always sat a bit crooked between his ears. Leo marched with his chest puffed out and paws on his hips, though he frequently tripped over his own cape.

"I am the Boss of the Jungle!" Leo boomed in a theatrical voice. "By my magnificent mane, everyone must obey me!"

Ruby, a small cream-colored rabbit in a bright red bowtie, watched Leo with a playful half-smirk. She tapped her left foot quickly. Leo was being far too bossy! Ruby reached into the gold pocket of her sky-blue utility belt and found a shiny pebble. She decided it was time for a clever prank.

"Oh, Great Leo!" Ruby squeaked, bouncing in a sudden burst of zoomies. "Wait for the wiggle!" She wiggled her nose rapidly. "I saw another lion! He says he is the real Boss. He claims he has a bigger mane and a much shinier crown than yours!"

Leo’s amber eyes widened. He let out a loud, dramatic gasp. "What? Another Boss? Impossible! Take me to him this instant!"

Ruby led Leo to a deep, stone well. "He is hiding down there, acting very scary," she whispered.

Leo marched to the edge and looked down. In the still water, he saw a grumpy lion with a crooked crown and a purple cape. Leo didn't realize he was looking at his own reflection!

"How dare you copy my royal style!" Leo roared. The echo roared back, sounding just as loud.

Leo was so impatient that he didn't stop to think. He puffed out his chest, leaped into the air, and—SPLASH! He landed right in the water.

Leo floated there, looking very wet and very silly. His purple cape floated around him like a giant grape. Ruby giggled, her chocolate-brown eyes twinkling.

"You see, Leo," Ruby laughed, "you don't need to be the Boss to be special. You just need to be kind!"

Leo sighed, a small bubble popping on his nose. He decided that being a friend was much better than being a bossy king.

The Storyteller closed his book with a wide, welcoming smile. "And that is how a tiny rabbit taught a big lion a very big lesson!"


MORAL

Being smart and kind is much more powerful than being big and bossy.
