TITLE

Ricky's Giant Shadow


STORY

Buster the lion stood on a hill and roared. "I am Buster, the biggest and strongest king of this forest!" he boomed.

Up in an oak tree, Pip the squirrel munched on a crunchy acorn. He loved eating snacks while watching the funny forest dramas unfold below him. "Oh boy, another show!" Pip squeaked, tossing an acorn shell.

Ricky the rabbit hopped by, shaking his long ears. "You may be big, Buster, but my shadow can grow much bigger than yours!" Ricky chirped.

Buster laughed so hard his gold mane shook. "A tiny rabbit with a giant shadow? Impossible!"

"Let’s have a contest at sunset," Ricky proposed. "Whoever has the biggest shadow is the cleverest animal in the forest."

"Deal!" roared Buster. Pip clapped his tiny paws in excitement.

As the sun began to sink, the forest turned golden. Pip sat on a low branch, ready to judge. "Showtime!" he chattered.

Buster stood flat on the grassy valley. The low evening sun cast his lion shadow behind him. It was long, but it was not very tall. "Ha! Look at my great shadow!" Buster bragged, puffing out his furry chest. "Beat that, little rabbit!"

Ricky smiled. He knew a secret about light. He hopped onto a tall, flat rock that was very close to the edge of the hill, right where the sun was shining. He stood on his tiptoes and stretched his long ears high.

Because Ricky was high up and blocking the sunlight right at the edge, his shadow stretched all the way across the grassy valley. It looked like a giant, super-rabbit with ears as tall as trees!

Pip gasped, dropping his acorn. "Wow! Ricky’s shadow is huge!"

Buster rubbed his eyes. He tried to jump to make his shadow bigger, but he just tumbled into a soft bush. "How did you do that?" Buster gasped. "Are you a wizard?"

"No," Ricky laughed. "It is just science! When an object blocks light, it makes a shadow. Because I stood high up and blocked the sun's low rays, my shadow stretched super far! Brains can make anything look big."

Buster smiled and shook Ricky’s paw. "You really are clever, Ricky. I guess size isn't everything!"

Pip cheered, "Hooray for science and smart rabbits!"


MORAL

Being clever and using your brain is much more powerful than just being big and strong.
