TITLE

Rudy's Giant Shadow


STORY

Deep in the green jungle lived Leo the lion. Leo was very grumpy and loud. "I am the biggest and the best!" Leo roared every morning. "I am the boss of everyone because I have the biggest shadow!"

Rudy was a tiny rabbit with big ears and even bigger ideas. Rudy did not like Leo’s bragging. He wanted to teach Leo a fun lesson about science and cleverness.

"Excuse me, Leo," Rudy squeaked one sunny morning. "I bet my shadow can be bigger than yours!"

Leo laughed so hard his tummy shook. "Ha! You are as small as a carrot, Rudy! My shadow is like a giant blanket. You cannot beat me."

"Let us meet at the Great Hill when the sun goes down," Rudy said with a tiny smile. "If my shadow is bigger, you must stop being grumpy."

"Deal!" roared Leo, confident he would win.

All day, the sun marched across the sky. Rudy waited patiently. He knew a secret about light. When the sun is high at noon, shadows are very short. But when the sun gets low at sunset, shadows stretch and grow super long!

At five o'clock, the sun was low in the west. Leo stood at the bottom of the Great Hill. His shadow was long, stretching across the grass. "See?" Leo boasted. "My shadow is huge!"

But Rudy was clever. He hopped all the way to the top of the Great Hill, right in front of the setting sun. Because Rudy was high up and close to the light source, his shadow stretched all the way down the hill, over the trees, and right past Leo! It looked like a giant monster rabbit.

Leo gasped. He looked at Rudy’s giant shadow, then at his own. "How did you do that?" Leo whispered, totally amazed.

"It is science!" Rudy giggled. "When you stand close to a low light, your shadow stretches. You do not have to be big to cast a giant shadow, Leo. You just have to know how light works!"

Leo smiled. For the first time, he was not grumpy. "You are very smart, Rudy," Leo said. "Can you teach me more?"

From that day on, Leo stopped bossing everyone around. He and Rudy became best friends, playing with shadows every single evening.


MORAL

Being clever and using your brain is much more powerful than just being big and loud.
