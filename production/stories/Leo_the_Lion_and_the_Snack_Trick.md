# Leo the Lion and the Snack Trick

## Story

Once upon a time, in a bright green forest, lived Leo the Lion. Leo was very big. He was very grumpy. But most of all, Leo loved snacks! He ate crunchy beetles, sweet berries, and yellow bananas. 'I am the King!' Leo roared, his tummy rumbling. 'All the snacks belong to me!' The other animals were scared of Leo. Except for Ricky the Rabbit. Ricky was tiny, but he was super smart. He wore a little red bowtie and always had a clever plan. One sunny morning, Leo stomped through the forest. 'I want a giant snack!' he grumbled. He saw Ricky holding a juicy carrot. 'Give me that carrot!' Leo ordered. Ricky did not run away. Instead, he smiled and bowed. 'Good morning, King Leo,' Ricky said politely. 'I would love to give you my carrot. But I am worried about the other lion.' Leo stopped. He puffed out his furry chest. 'Another lion? In my forest? No way!' 'Oh, yes!' Ricky squeaked. 'He is huge. He is super grumpy. And he says he is the real King of Snacks! He is hiding down in the old stone well.' Leo’s face turned red. 'Show me!' he roared. Ricky led Leo to the deep, dark well at the edge of the forest. Leo marched up to the edge and looked down into the water. Down in the well, Leo saw a lion looking back at him. It was just his own reflection, but Leo did not know that! 'Hey! You are ugly!' Leo yelled down the well. The reflection looked like it was yelling back. Leo shook his big mane. The reflection shook its mane, too. 'I am the only King of Snacks!' Leo roared. The well made his roar sound even louder: 'ROAR!' Leo got so angry that his ears wiggled. 'That is it! I am coming down!' Leo yelled. With a giant leap, Leo jumped right into the well. SPLASH! Water flew everywhere. Leo got soaked from his nose to his tail. He looked around, but the other lion was gone! Ricky looked down and laughed. 'Do you see him, Leo?' Leo sneezed. 'Achoo! No, only cold water!' Ricky smiled. 'The other lion was just you, Leo! You got tricked!' Leo looked at his wet paws and started to laugh. It was a very funny trick. Ricky helped Leo climb out of the well using a long vine. From that day on, Leo was not grumpy anymore. He became a nice king, and he always shared his snacks with his clever friend, Ricky.

---

## Moral

Being clever is much better than just being big and strong.
