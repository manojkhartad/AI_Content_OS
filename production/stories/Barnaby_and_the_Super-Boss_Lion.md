# Barnaby and the Super-Boss Lion

## Story

In the sunny-leaf forest, Buster the lion loved to strut. He wore a snug royal blue vest and a tiny gold crown that always sat crookedly on his head. Buster had a very big voice and an even bigger ego. He had collected every single toy in the playground—all the balls, hoops, and blocks—into one giant pile. "These are all mine because I'm the Boss, that's why!" he boomed, puffing out his chest.

Barnaby, a small rabbit in a lemon-yellow star t-shirt and royal blue shorts, watched from behind a bush. He adjusted his round red glasses and tapped his chin. "This is a puzzle that needs solving," Barnaby whispered. Beside him, Pip the squirrel twitched his nose and fidgeted with his yellow knitted scarf. "Are we sure about this?" Pip squeaked, clutching his teal satchel. "Buster is very loud!"

Barnaby smiled confidently and stood with his hands on his hips. "Let's think this through!" he exclaimed. Barnaby knew Buster was easily distracted by things that looked important. He hopped over to the lion with a look of great surprise.

"Mr. Buster!" Barnaby cried. "I just saw another lion near the old stone well. He is huge, he has a shiny crown, and he says he is the REAL Boss of the playground!"

Buster’s emerald eyes widened. He gasped a loud, squeaky gasp. "Impossible! No one is the boss but me!" He began a pompous, high-stepping strut toward the well, nearly tripping over his own paws.

When they reached the well, Barnaby pointed downward. "He's hiding right there!" 

Buster leaned over the edge. He saw a chubby lion with a gold crown staring back at him. Not realizing it was his own reflection, Buster let out a theatrical roar. The reflection roared back! Infuriated, Buster jumped into the well with a giant SPLASH!

Buster floated to the top, soaking wet and looking like a soggy orange pom-pom. His tiny crown drifted beside him. He wasn't feeling very bossy anymore. "Maybe sharing the toys is better," he whispered sheepishly.

Barnaby and Pip helped him out of the water. The toys were returned to everyone, and the playground was happy once again.

---

## Moral

Being smart and thinking clearly is much more powerful than being bossy or strong.
