TITLE

The Lion, The Well, and The Wiggle


STORY

Leo the Lion stood on a mossy rock, his royal purple velvet cape fluttering behind him. His gold crown, as always, was tilted comically to the left. He puffed out his chest, his giant orange mane looking like a fuzzy sunburst. 'Attention, everyone!' he boomed, his voice raspy and theatrical. 'I am the Boss of the Brush! Now, who is going to bring me a snack?'

All the jungle animals hid behind trees. They were tired of Leo’s grumpiness. Suddenly, Ruby the Rabbit hopped into the clearing. She wore her favorite teal overalls, and the red ribbon on her ear bounced with every step. She wasn't scared; she was analytical. She saw Leo trip over his own cape and frown at a passing butterfly, which made him shriek and scramble up a tree.

Ruby tapped her chin. 'Got a bright idea!' she chirped. She hopped over to the grumpy lion. 'Your Majesty, there is a terrible intruder in the old stone well. He says he is the real Boss of the Brush!'

Leo’s eyes went wide. He jumped down, smoothing his cape with a huff. 'What? A challenger? Impossible! Take me to him at once!'

Ruby led Leo to the deep, circular well. 'Look down there, Leo. He’s hiding in the water!'

Leo stomped to the edge and peered inside. He saw a fluffy orange mane, a gold crown, and a cute, scowling face staring back. 'How dare you!' Leo roared, his voice echoing off the stone walls. The reflection roared back. Leo was so frustrated by the 'imposter' that he decided to show him who was truly in charge. With a dramatic leap, Leo dove into the well with a giant, soaking SPLASH!

Luckily, the water was very shallow and cool. Leo stood up, dripping wet and looking very silly. The 'imposter' was gone, replaced by bubbles. Ruby giggled, her nose wiggling with delight. 'Oh, Leo! That wasn't a challenger. That was just you!'

Leo looked at his soggy reflection and sighed. He realized that being a boss didn't mean scaring everyone; it meant being kind. He climbed out, shook his mane like a wet dog, and apologized to the animals. From that day on, Leo practiced being a better leader, though he still needed Ruby’s help to solve his daily puzzles—mostly involving where he left his crown.


MORAL

Being clever and using your brain is always better than being bossy and using your strength.
